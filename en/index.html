Approach

Calculation and drawing handled as one workflow.

Model and calculations developed together

The BIM model and the calculation report draw on the same data. This common basis removes any gap between the model, the calculations and the drawings issued to site.

Quantities and cost estimate

Preliminary quantities at design stage, detailed quantity take-off from the construction drawings, schedules, then the cost estimate.

The clause, always cited

Every check carries the reference of the clause it relies on. A calculation report must be open to review and challenge, clause by clause.

Explicit assumptions

Allowable bearing pressure, concrete class, concrete cover, seismic category: the assumptions are stated at the top of the report.

Traceability

Dated revisions, recorded assumptions, readable calculation sheets. A calculation that cannot be picked up again six months later has no value.
