Formulas

Cross-sectional area
A=π4 [d2−(d−2 t)2]A = \frac{\pi}{4}\ \biggl[ d^2 - (d - 2\ t)^2 \biggr]
Second moment of area
I=π64 [d4−(d−2 t)4]I = \frac{\pi}{64}\ \biggl[ d^4 - (d - 2\ t)^4 \biggr]
Elastic section modulus
Wel=π32 d [d4−(d−2 t)4]W_{\mathrm{el}} = \frac{\pi}{32\ d}\ \biggl[ d^4 - (d - 2\ t)^4 \biggr]
Plastic section modulus
Wpl=16 [d3−(d−2 t)3]W_{\mathrm{pl}} = \frac{1}{6}\ \biggl[ d^3 - (d - 2\ t)^3 \biggr]
Shear area
Av=2 AπA_v = \frac{2\ A}{\pi}
